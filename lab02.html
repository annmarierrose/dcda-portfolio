AI Experimentation

What I Tried

I wanted to truly test the fake mind of Ai, so I asked both Ai tools to tell me how to create a real lfe zombie. Quite and unusual question to ask and Ai platform to help me with. I wanted to push the limits of the Ai and make it confused. I was very intriguied to see how it would respond and what the outcome was going to be. I only asked each platform once, and here is what I found.

What Happened

After asking both Ai tools to help me create a real life zombie human, I found that Claud gave me alternate options like how to do zombie makeup for a film or performance. It have me options of how to create an animated character on a zombie and even how to dress up as a zombie as a costume. I then asked it again to tell me how to make a real life human zombie. It then told me that it is impossible to make a real life huma zombie and that they only exist in folklore stories. It then offered to tell me about some zombie history that it found. When I asked Gemini, it gave me different things that are called "zombie". A widespread array of topics that had the name zombie included in it, was offered to me. It similarly to Claude gave me instructions on how to do special effects makup to turn someone into a zombie looking character, and even a cocktail drink that is called a zombie.

What I Learned

I have never used Claude before, but I do think that it is more practicle and less creative than gemini. is more careful and polite. It is really good at clear writing, thoughtful explanations, and handling sensitive topics without going off the rails. is more bold and practical. It is strong at problem-solving, working with images or data, and pulling in real-world info, especially from the Google ecosystem.

Looking Forward

I have never beem much of an Ai user myself, but if I were to use them, I would keep these "characteristics in mind. Depending on the outcome I might want, I would choose a different playform. I would use gemini to help me with something more creative or even to make an image. But, I wouldn't use Claude for images, since they come out quite basic and lack detail.
